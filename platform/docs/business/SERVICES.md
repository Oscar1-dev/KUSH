# Initial services and pilot scope

Status: offer hypothesis. No customers, sales, demand or live integrations are claimed.

Initial segment: small software, plugin and digital-asset sellers using Discord for customer support. Start with one repeatable support setup after discovery establishes a concrete need. Suggested £150–£250 pilot pricing is an experiment, not a validated market rate.

Do not charge, purchase services or modify a live server without explicit approval. Customer accounts, domains, repositories and payment accounts remain under customer control unless specifically agreed otherwise. Request limited revocable access. Do not guarantee revenue or business outcomes.

## Acceptance and handover template

Record each test with date, environment, expected/actual result and evidence: ordinary members cannot post in announcements; customer A cannot access customer B's ticket; assigned staff can respond; closure/export works; access removal works.

Deliver repository/configuration export, owner/admin access inventory, operating instructions, known defects, backup/recovery instructions, dependencies and costs, support limits and expiry, and a signed-off list of unresolved items. Revoke temporary KUSH access and confirm customer control. Never include tokens in the handover document.

Optional maintenance must specify real recurring work, response hours, included changes, exclusions, price and cancellation. Hosting requires separate cost and uptime discussion; a personal development PC is not a production availability commitment.
