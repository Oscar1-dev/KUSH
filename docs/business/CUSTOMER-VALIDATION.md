Seek ten relevant conversations as an initial learning target. If no repeated costly problem or willingness to buy emerges, revise the offer before adding platform scope. A numerical target is a decision aid, not proof of demand. Use permitted community channels and individual permission; no scraping contact lists or automated unsolicited messaging.

For each pilot record agreed scope, actual delivery hours, revisions, costs, defects, usage and customer feedback. Obtain separate permission for any public case study. Productise only when repeated paid engagements share a workflow and maintenance economics are credible. Revenue examples and forecasts must be explicitly labelled assumptions.
