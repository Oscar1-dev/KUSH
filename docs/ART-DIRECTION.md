# KUSH visual direction

**Positioning:** premium, provocative, underground-inspired digital studio with technical credibility.

**Design language:** near-black editorial canvas, oversized compressed typography, acidic botanical green, deep purple haze, deliberate white space, irregular organic geometry, layered motion, precise transitions.

**Motion language:** momentum, inertia, magnetic hover, cursor-responsive depth, scroll-linked reveals, dynamic type, occasional liquid/smoke-inspired transitions. Avoid motion for its own sake.

**Hero:** dramatic two-line headline, oversized green second line, atmospheric purple orb/rings, reactive light, quiet technical labels, directional scroll affordance.

**Reference asset:** original KUSH graffiti logo is NOT bundled. Add only an owned/licensed image under `public/brand/` when available. Do not copy or approximate it without the source.

**Rules:** no generic AI SaaS templates; no fake product cards or statistics; don't market unbuilt features as launched; ensure accessible interaction and a fast first load.
